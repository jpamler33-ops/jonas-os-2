## 1. Purpose

This migration recovers the historical BIGGJ / TCX idea space without pretending that one perfectly preserved original "~150 ideas" list exists.

Two source classes are preserved separately:

1. **TCX WORLD — Master Knowledge Base**  
   Exact historical DEC/REQ records recovered from the user-owned Notion workspace.

2. **BIGGJ chat-history recovery**  
   Named concepts recovered from the September 27–29, 2026 BIGGJ design conversations.

The migration keeps source identity and recovery confidence so reconstructed chat concepts are never silently presented as exact Notion records.

Historical existence is not a reason for present-day inclusion.

## 6. Fifteen ideas that survive as real research proposals

These concepts are not promoted merely because they are historically interesting.

They survive as governed research proposals because the current canon does not fully subsume them.

### 6.1 LATENT_MARKET_ENERGY

Parent: FORCED_FLOW

Research a bounded diagnostic for unreleased constrained flow weighted by:

- proximity,
- urgency,
- latent notional,
- liquidity impact.

Hard semantic rule:

> "Energy" is a model diagnostic, not physical energy and not a probability.

### 6.2 PRICE_TIME_CONSTRAINT_SURFACE

Parent: CONSTRAINT_MAP

Represent:

`price × time × constrained flow × confidence`

with explicit temporal decay.

### 6.3 MINIMUM_CASCADE_TRIGGER

Parent: CASCADE_DYNAMICS

Estimate the smallest bounded finite shock that reaches a preregistered cascade criterion.

### 6.4 CASCADE_BASIN

Parent: CASCADE_DYNAMICS

Map:

`shock × liquidity state → absorption / criticality / amplification`

### 6.5 ABSORPTION_RESERVE

Parent: ABSORPTION

Estimate how much additional liquidity/counterflow would be required to keep a defined cascade below a target reproduction level.

### 6.6 MECHANISM_ENSEMBLE

Parent: IDENTIFIABILITY

Run multiple plausible hidden-mechanism reconstructions and expose:

- agreement,
- dispersion,
- ambiguity,
- underidentification.

### 6.7 MECHANISM_POSTERIOR_GRAPH

Parent: IDENTIFIABILITY

Represent a posterior over plausible mechanism graphs instead of choosing one hidden story.

### 6.8 REFLEXIVITY_WITNESS_ENSEMBLE

Parent: REFLEXIVITY

Combine methodologically distinct witnesses such as:

- finite-shock reproduction,
- local feedback/Jacobian diagnostics,
- self-excitation,
- post-trigger flow persistence,
- liquidity withdrawal,
- cross-venue propagation,
- observed liquidation chains.

Method lineage must prevent fake consensus.

### 6.9 INTERVENTION_VALUE_MAP

Parent: INFORMATION_VALUE_PRIORITIZATION

Ask:

> Which next observation or stress test would reduce mechanism uncertainty the most?

This connects mechanism research with value-of-information.

### 6.10 CAUSAL_EPISODE_MEMORY

Parent: POST_TRADE_REVERSE_ENGINEERING

Store mechanism-centered episodes containing:

- initial state,
- active constraints,
- trigger,
- flow generations,
- liquidity reaction,
- peak reproduction,
- absorption/cascade outcome,
- later realized outcome.

Strict-past retrieval remains mandatory.

### 6.11 CLAIM_ASSUMPTION_GRAPH

Parent: PROVENANCE_CHAIN

Track:

`Claim → Assumptions → Evidence → Dependencies → Falsifiers`

This makes hidden assumption load measurable.

### 6.12 LEAVE_ONE_OUT_ROBUSTNESS

Parent: SCIENTIFIC_GUARD_ORCHESTRATION

Re-run conclusions after removing:

- one evidence lineage,
- one feature family,
- one market,
- one regime,
- one winner,
- one methodological family.

The purpose is fragility detection, not automatic proof.

### 6.13 MEME_RUG_RISK_INTELLIGENCE

Parent: MEME_MOMENTUM

Keep rug/manipulation/liquidity-fragility research isolated from general memecoin-momentum evidence.

This prevents a "meme strategy" from pretending that asset-integrity risk is already solved.

### 6.14 GOVERNED_EXPERIMENT_BLUEPRINTS

Parent: EXPERIMENT_DESIGN

Historical source concepts:

- Research Process Genome
- Experiment Blueprint DSL

Preserve the useful part:

bounded declarative research/experiment blueprints.

Reject the dangerous interpretation:

arbitrary autonomous code self-modification.

### 6.15 RESEARCH_POLICY_GENOME

Parent: VERSIONED_CANDIDATES

Represent bounded research-policy candidates as explicit versioned challengers.

Examples of mutable policy parameters may include:

- research allocation,
- experiment ordering,
- evidence thresholds,
- stopping policies,
- diversity budgets.

Immutable scientific/safety controls remain outside this optimization surface.

## 11. Migration priority model

Implementation priority is not determined by nostalgia or how impressive an idea sounds.

Priority should be driven by:

`scientific necessity × uncertainty reduction × dependency leverage × downstream unlocks × mission relevance`

The dependency graph therefore has priority over the historical feature list.

For example:

A visually impressive old feature should not outrank PROVENANCE_CHAIN if provenance blocks dozens of scientific capabilities.

## 12. Next step

Do **not** implement all fifteen proposals at once.

The next step should be a **Historical Proposal Research Gate**:

For each of the fifteen proposals generate:

1. research question,
2. falsifiable hypothesis,
3. explicit falsifier,
4. parent skill,
5. hard/support/guard dependencies,
6. required data,
7. baseline comparison,
8. PIT requirements,
9. leakage/selection/confounding risks,
10. minimum forward-shadow design,
11. kill criteria,
12. strategic leverage,
13. expected information value.

Then rank the proposals by dependency leverage and information gain.

Only the highest-value proposal should enter active implementation first.
